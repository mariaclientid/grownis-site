What we do

We point our full-funnel growth toolkit at one job: getting the right technical audience to your event — and keeping them after it.

Foundation

Check every touchpoint before launch — site, landing page, social channels, and registration flow — and fix the leaks before traffic starts flowing.

Output: a touchpoint audit and a registration funnel that works.

Creative & Content Production

Define what to say, to whom, and when — across participants, partners, sponsors and tech communities. Then turn the strategy into campaign assets: key visual, campaign creative, and content tailored to every channel and every stage of the event.

Output: a messaging platform, a communication roadmap, a creative kit, and a cross-channel content plan.

Promotion

Launch campaigns that reach the right technical audience through paid media, PR, influencers, partners, and community channels. Activate partner reach and distribute event content where your audience already is.

Output: a channel plan, live campaigns (paid, PR, influencers), and event-content distribution.

Audience Lifecycle

Registration isn't attendance. Keep people engaged with pre-event nurture, reminders, and waitlist flows to maximise show-rate — the metric marketing actually owns. Then turn a one-off event into a returning audience through post-event communication and community building.

Output: a lifecycle communication system that increases attendance and compounds into a long-term community.

Measurement

A layer that runs through every stage of the event funnel — from promotion to registration, attendance, and qualified leads. Measure what matters, spot bottlenecks early, and continuously improve performance.

Output: an event funnel and the key metrics worth watching.

Case — Agentic RAG Challenge 2026

An international legal AI engineering competition held during Dubai AI Week in partnership with the Machines Can See conference. Participants built production-grade Agentic RAG systems evaluated on real legal documents from DIFC under benchmark conditions rather than hackathon-style demos. The challenge was positioned within the UAE's broader push to become a global leader in Agentic AI adoption, with government-backed initiatives accelerating AI deployment across public services. The objective was to attract the world's strongest technical teams within a two-week promotion window.

What we did

  • Positioned the challenge for a narrow, expert audience and mapped where those builders actually are.
  • Ran acquisition where they spend time: targeted Telegram seeding, paid media, outreach to specialist communities and universities, and partners' publications.
  • Built the analytics and lead-capture funnel to track the journey from first touch to registration.
  • Launched a welcome email sequence and lifecycle campaigns across email and social — from registration and reminders to submission deadlines and the final event.
  • Developed the Discord communication strategy, editorial calendar, and content to keep participants engaged throughout.
  • Turned the final event into its own media moment.
